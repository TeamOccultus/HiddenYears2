Welcome to the land beneath the Scorchwall, Voyager!

A unified nation once stood here, but time and the gods have turned it all to dust and sand.

On your first entry, this land may feel desolate and lonely—but as you delve deeper, the secrets hidden within the world will gradually be revealed...

## Overview

> You awaken from a lonely slumber; countless storms have worn even the hardest stone to dust.
>
> Traverse the enchanting landscapes, explore the unknown world.
>
> Here and now, the vast world is yours to explore; past and future, history shall be written by you.
